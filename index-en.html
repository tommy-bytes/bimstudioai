Model the building once

BIM Studio AI is a parametric building information modelling tool for architects. The plans, the 3D view, the door, window and room schedules, the take-off, the PDF drawing set and the IFC file are all derived from the same model, so they cannot fall out of step with it.

What it does

  • Walls with layered build-ups, doors and windows hosted on their wall, slabs, rooms and storeys, drawn with ranked snapping and live dimensions.
  • Real-time 3D built from the same walls, with the openings cut through them.
  • Quantities and costs net of openings and of the plan two walls share at a corner, per layer at that layer's own rate, in the project's own currency. What cannot be measured is reported as unmeasured, never as zero.
  • A model checker with a health score and a delivery gate that takes you to the element it is about.
  • Vector PDF sheets from A0 to A4 at 1:20 to 1:500 with a professional title block, and IFC 4 for openBIM exchange.
  • A design assistant that reads a description of the building and proposes a plan you accept or reject. It runs entirely on your device.

Subscription

The app is free to download and free to explore: open the example, move through every screen, edit it, and see exactly what it does. BIM Studio AI Pro is the subscription that lets you use it on your own projects.

Details in the terms of use and the privacy policy.

Privacy

No account and no sign-in. Nothing about your work is collected. Your projects are files on your own device. The only connection the app makes is to the App Store, to check your subscription. See the privacy policy and the terms of use.

What it is not

BIM Studio AI models architecture. It does not do structural analysis or services design, and it does not pretend to. Its output is design information for a licensed professional to verify, not a substitute for that verification.
