Privacy Policy

BIM Studio AI collects no data and has no network access. Your projects are files on your own device, and only you move them.

Version 1.0 · effective 7 September 2026 · versiunea în română

1. In short

  • The app has no network access. It sends nothing anywhere and downloads nothing. The developer operates no server for this app.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs and no advertising identifier.
  • Projects — the walls, openings, slabs, rooms and the project and team details you type — are written only on the device, in the app's own container.
  • Payment is a one-time purchase through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details.

2. Who the controller is

The developer, MSCS di Stefan E., processes no personal data through this app, because it never receives any. Whatever you type — for instance the architect's or client's name in a title block — stays on your device and in the files you choose to export. For that data, you are the controller.

4. The design assistant

The description you type is read on the device. On iOS 26 and macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, it is read by the system's own language model running locally through Apple's Foundation Models framework; on other systems, by a built-in vocabulary. In neither case is the text sent to any server, the developer's or anyone else's.

5. Files you open

When you import a .bimproj file, the app reads it once and copies it into its container. It keeps no access to the original file and reads no other files.

6. Payment

The app is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive card details, billing address or the buyer's identity — only the aggregated sales reports Apple provides.

7. Retention

As long as you keep it. Deleting a project in the app deletes its file and its backup. Uninstalling the app deletes its whole container. Files you exported stay wherever you put them.

8. Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, rectify or erase on request. For anything about the app itself, write to stefan@support-remote.org.

9. Children

The app is a professional tool for architects and is not directed at children. It contains no objectionable content and collects data from nobody, of any age.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the version and effective date at the top of this page are updated. Earlier versions remain in the public history of this site's code repository.
